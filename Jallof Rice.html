Jallof Rice

This is a flavorful nigerian made jallof rice mix with Chicken, lime and different cabbage to give it that nutritious taste.

List of Ingredients Needed to prepare this Meal

  • Rice
  • Green Pie
  • Salt
  • Maggi
  • Pepper
  • Cray Fish
  • Grandnunt Oil
  • Chicken Meat
  • Water

    How To Prepare this Jallof Rice

  1. Parboile your rice and Wash it
  2. Put and empty Pot on the fire add your oil then allow to steam, then add Ingredients while frying
  3. Allow to fry for 5mins then add water and add the rice allow to cook till done
  4. Fry the Chicken separately
