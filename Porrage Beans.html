Porrage Beans

This is a Nigerian made porrage beans for local consumption. It is combined with fish and fried plantain

List of Ingredients Needed to prepare this Meal

  • Beans
  • Fish
  • Salt
  • Maggi
  • Pepper
  • Cray Fish
  • Grandnunt Oil
  • Plantain
  • Water
  • Onions

    How To Prepare this Porrage Beans

  1. Boil your Beans on fire for it to cook
  2. In another separate pot, warm up your red oil, add onions and other condiments.
  3. After adding all condiments, add yor beans and stare for a while
  4. Add your fired fish and allow for 1mins then bring your beans down
  5. Fry the plantain separately
